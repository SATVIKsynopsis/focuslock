    Good to know

    • Off-list apps bounce back to the last allowed app you used. Finder, Spotlight and System Settings always work.
    • Really need something? 🎯 → "I need something else…", type a reason, and get a short pass.
    • "Stop session" is the no-questions emergency exit. Quit during a session asks for a reason.
    • Settings saved during a session apply to your next one.
    • Everything stays on your Mac. No account, no tracking. Stats come from a local log file.
